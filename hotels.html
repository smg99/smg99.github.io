The opportunity

A hotel website should do more than look good.

Guests may find you on MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Google or Instagram. Your own website should turn that attention into confidence, enquiries and direct bookings — while the hotel gets a clearer view of what is happening behind the scenes.

OTA dependenceOTAs are useful distribution channels, but your own website can become another direct channel.
Manual coordinationRooms, dates and guest requests should not live entirely inside scattered conversations.
Disconnected toolsThe guest experience and the hotel's availability workflow should work together.
That is the direction we've been building with Jenvin.

A practical distinction

Calendar sync is useful. It is not a channel manager.

Calendar feeds can reduce manual availability updates, but they should not be described as real-time two-way OTA connectivity.

Our rule

We only call an integration “live” when it is actually connected, authenticated, tested and working end-to-end.

For deeper OTA, PMS, payment or social integrations, we check the provider's supported capabilities before promising them.
