The opportunity

A hotel website should do more than look good.

Guests may find you on MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Airbnb, Google or Instagram. Your own website should turn that attention into confidence, enquiries and direct bookings — while the hotel gets a clear, unified view of availability behind the scenes.

OTA dependence OTAs are useful discovery channels, but direct bookings belong on your own front door.
Manual coordination Rooms, dates and guest requests shouldn't live scattered across chat threads and paper logs.
Disconnected tools The guest booking journey and the host's daily availability calendar should work together seamlessly.
That is the direction we built into Jenvin.
A beautiful guest experience + real-time availability + reservation tracking + owner/host operations, without forcing you into costly legacy PMS lock-ins.

A practical distinction

Calendar sync is useful. It is not a channel manager.

Calendar feeds can significantly reduce manual availability updates, but they should never be mislabeled as real-time two-way OTA synchronization.

Our rule

We only describe an integration as “live” when it is authenticated, tested, and working end-to-end with supported APIs.

For deeper OTA, PMS, payment, or messaging integrations, we verify the provider's actual technical capabilities before promising them in scope.
