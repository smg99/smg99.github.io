Common questions

Clear answers.
Before we build.

Practical answers to typical questions from hotel & villa operators, startup founders, and engineering teams.

How does the hotel direct booking & WhatsApp flow work?

We build mobile-first hospitality websites with live room showcase, seasonal availability calendars, and direct WhatsApp reservation journeys. Guests select suites, confirm travel dates, and enquiry details route directly to your host's WhatsApp. You capture direct guest relationships, collect deposits upfront, and avoid 15%–25% OTA commissions without paying costly PMS monthly fees.

What types of AI products and custom software do you build?

Full-stack web applications, autonomous digital workers, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and LLM-powered interfaces. Everything is engineered with deterministic fallbacks, clean API boundaries, and responsive interfaces designed to compound in utility over time.

Can you migrate an existing hotel or business website?

Yes. We regularly modernize slow or outdated WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and custom websites. We preserve your existing domain authority, upgrade page load speed to sub-second benchmarks, optimize local SEO, and configure custom domains and hosting cleanly.

What are typical turnaround times and project milestones?

We adhere strictly to our "ship early" ethos. Most hospitality websites and focused product MVPs launch within 1 to 3 weeks. You get hands-on staging reviews early in the process so reality shapes the build rather than lengthy theoretical planning.

Are there recurring platform fees or code lock-in?

No. You own 100% of your codebase, digital assets, and custom domain setup. For hotel builds, we prioritize lightweight architectures that run at near-zero maintenance overhead rather than locking you into expensive monthly SaaS subscriptions.

How do we start a discussion about our project?

Drop a quick message on WhatsApp or email with your current website link, property details, or product concept. We'll review your scope, propose a sensible implementation plan, and answer any technical questions upfront.
