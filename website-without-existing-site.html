Can a business build a website without already having one?

Yes. A first website concept can begin with the business name, location, services, public business profile and social presence. Public information is useful for research, but final facts should be verified by the owner and photos, logos and other assets should be owned or authorized before publication.

What matters most

The right decision depends on the business goal, not on adding technology for its own sake. Keep the experience fast, mobile-friendly, easy to understand and easy to act on.

What should you prepare?

Start with accurate business details, services or rooms, contact information, brand assets and photos you own or are authorized to use. Then add integrations only where they solve a real customer problem.
